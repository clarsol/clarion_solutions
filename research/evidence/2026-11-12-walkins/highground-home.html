HighGround Pest Defense is a locally owned, veteran-owned pest control company dedicated to protecting homes across Anna, Melissa, Van Alstyne, McKinney, and surrounding North Texas communities.

We protect homes and businesses from common pests like ants, spiders, roaches, rodents, termites, scorpions, and crickets using proven treatment methods and a proactive strategy designed to stop pest problems before they start.

Our TriGuard™ Protection Program takes this a step further by creating a strong defensive barrier around your home for long-lasting peace of mind.

Why Homeowners Choose HighGround Pest Defense

HighGround Pest Defense was built on the belief that pest control should be thorough, honest, and reliable.

Owner, Rex Butler, a U.S. Army veteran, founded HighGround to provide homeowners with a higher standard of pest protection.

We focus on exterior-first pest control strategies designed to help keep pests out - not just treat them after they get inside.
