Three opportunities worth fixing first

01
Show your real results.
Your homepage leads with a model photo, and the footer says images may contain models. For a med spa, the photos are the product. Consented before-and-afters and short injector introductions show what only you offer.
02
Decide what AI gets to read.
Your website turns away automated visitors and tells several AI companies' crawlers not to read it. Blocking AI training is a fair choice, but it means AI describes you from other sites instead: ChatGPT reported 183 reviews and Google's AI 157.
03
Check where Google sends people.
Your Google listing's Website button opens your booking page, and Google shows a different phone number than your site. Are both on purpose? If not, people comparing spas may never see your site.
