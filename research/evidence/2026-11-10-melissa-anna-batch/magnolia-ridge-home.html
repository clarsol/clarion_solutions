We Guide Patients on the Path to Sustainable Oral Health and Wellness

At Magnolia Ridge Dentistry, we provide comprehensive dental care for children and adults in Melissa, TX, with an approach that looks beyond symptoms to understand the underlying causes of oral health concerns. From pediatric dentistry that supports growing smiles to airway and sleep-focused care for patients of all ages, we consider how your oral health connects to your overall well-being.

Dr. Kathleen Schuseter and our team evaluate how the teeth, jaws, airway, and muscles of the face, mouth, throat, and tongue work together, and how these relationships may affect breathing, sleep, comfort, and daily life. Whether we’re caring for your child or helping you address your dental concerns, we create personalized treatment plans to support a healthier smile and better quality of life.

Kathleen Schuster, DDS

Dr. Schuster was raised in Mansfield, TX, and knew she wanted to be a dentist by age 4. She received her undergraduate degree in nutrition from Texas A&M University and her dental degree from the prestigious UT Health San Antonio School of Dentistry. Before establishing Magnolia Ridge Dentistry, Dr. Schuster worked at a pediatric dentistry practice for five years. This set in motion her dedication to helping children align their smiles, prevent the potentially long-term repercussions of tongue ties, restore airways and breathing, and improve their overall quality of life.

Dr. Schuster has completed advanced training in specialized areas, including implantology, full mouth reconstruction, and orthodontics for airway expansion. She is also passionate about enhancing patients’ smile aesthetics through cosmetic dentistry procedures, such as porcelain veneers and teeth whitening. Dr. Schuster maintains memberships in professional organizations, including the American Dental Association, Texas Dental Association, Dallas County Dental Society, and American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

Experience Enduring Optimal Wellness with Evidence-Based Treatments

Pediatric & Adult Airway Dentistry

Healthy breathing matters at every age, from your child’s earliest development through adulthood. We evaluate how the teeth, jaws, tongue, and oral tissues work together to identify concerns and create a personalized plan that supports breathing, oral function, and overall wellness.

Sleep Apnea Treatment

Snoring, restless nights, and daytime fatigue may indicate a breathing concern that warrants a closer look. Dr. Schuster works with you and your healthcare providers to evaluate your needs and explore personalized treatment options to help you achieve more restful sleep.

Airway Orthodontics

Orthodontic care can address more than the appearance of your smile. For children and adults, we evaluate jaw development, dental arch width, and bite alignment to determine whether airway-focused orthodontics could support better oral function and breathing.

CPAP Alternatives

If you struggle with CPAP, you may have other options for managing obstructive sleep apnea. Based on your diagnosis, oral anatomy, and individual needs, we explore alternatives designed to support nighttime breathing and fit your daily routine.

General Dentistry

A healthy smile starts with personalized care that keeps your teeth and gums strong through every stage of life. From routine exams and cleanings to restorative treatments, we help children and adults maintain comfortable, confident smiles while addressing concerns early.

Same-Day Crowns

Restore a damaged or weakened tooth with a custom crown designed and crafted right here in our office. Our CEREC® technology allows us to restore your smile in a single visit, without temporary crowns or a return appointment for placement.
