Meet Dr. Vinh Ngo

Dr. Vinh Ngo attended the University of California in Davis, California, where he earned his Bachelors of Science Degree in Genetics. He went on to attend dental school at Columbia University where he earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. He chose to become a dentist because he loves science and healthcare. Dr. Ngo also wants to make a difference every day and enjoys practicing dentistry because it gives him the opportunity to help patients achieve a beautiful smile.

While not practicing dentistry, Dr. Ngo likes to stay physically active by working out. He also enjoys basketball, traveling, and a good game of chess.

Meet Dr. Emilie Faucheu

Dr. Emilie Faucheu attended the University of Houston where she earned her Bachelor of Science Degree in Biology while minoring in chemistry. Dr. Faucheu went on to attend Midwestern University in Glendale, Arizona, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine Degree. Following in her father’s footsteps, Dr. Faucheu chose to be a dentist because she found the diversity in dentistry fascinating. She enjoys the daily interactions with her patients and making them happy!

In her spare time, Dr. Faucheu likes to work out. She also enjoys cooking and eating her food creations.
