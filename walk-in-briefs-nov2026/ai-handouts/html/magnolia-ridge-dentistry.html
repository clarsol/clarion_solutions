Three opportunities worth fixing first

01
Put your videos where Google looks.
Your YouTube channel has 9 subscribers and nothing new in a year, while the same kind of video earns 100,000 views on Instagram. Google owns YouTube and AI tools quote it. Your best reels belong there, on your Google listing and on your website.
02
Bring your airway brand home.
Your Instagram calls you a kids' airway dentist in the McKinney area. When we asked Google's AI for a pediatric airway dentist near McKinney, it named four other practices and not you. AI can't see the audience you've built on Instagram.
03
Answer your reviews.
None of your 8 newest Google reviews has a reply, including a 1-star from last week. Google's AI now mentions recent concerns about follow-up when it describes you, and a reply is the one place your side of the story shows up.
