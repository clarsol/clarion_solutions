North Texas plumbing questions

What plumbing problems should I call about first?

Call first for active leaks, sewage backup, sewer odor, no hot water, water near flooring or walls, and any issue that may cause property damage.

Yes. Masters Touch helps with residential plumbing and light commercial service calls such as restrooms, drains, leaks, water heaters, utility sinks, and property-side symptoms.
