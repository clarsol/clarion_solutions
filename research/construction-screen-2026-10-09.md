Source: Google Maps searches run 2026-10-09: "general contractor", "remodeling contractor" and "custom home builder" + city TX. Addresses, ratings and review counts come from the search result lists. Only the first 3 towns' candidates were opened individually.

This is a storefront check only. No media or marketing claims have been verified for any of these businesses.

Excluded: national and production builders (Lennar, KB, Drees, Grand, Coventry, First Texas, Tilson, Windsor, Cope, Cavender, Grenadier), addresses on residential streets, and listings with no address.

**Mailbox warning:** an address like "Ste 110-224" or "Suite 500 #544" is almost always a mailbox (for example a UPS Store), not an office. Some single suites in office parks (Granite Pkwy, Lake Forest Dr, Preston Rd Ste 400) may be shared or virtual offices. Call ahead before going to any of the "check first" rows.
