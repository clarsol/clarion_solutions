## How AI search works (plain-English explainer for owners)
Use this in conversation, or drop it into any handout.

- **What's changing.** Customers increasingly ask an AI ("Who's the best AC company in Melissa?") instead of scrolling a list.
  - Google now shows an AI Overview on **39.4% of U.S. desktop searches** (June 2026, up from 25.8% in July 2025). Source: Comscore Q2 2026 AI Intelligence Report, Sept. 22, 2026. That's vendor research and covers desktop only.
  - Comscore also reports the use is spreading beyond ChatGPT to Gemini, Claude and others.
- **What the AI does.** It reads your Google listing, your reviews, directories (Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Birdeye) and your website, then **names two to five businesses**. Customers often call one of those names without ever seeing the rest of the list.
- **What it rewards:**
  1. The same facts everywhere (name, address, phone, hours).
  2. Clear answers on your own site ("Do you take my insurance?", "Are you open 24/7?").
  3. Reviews on more than just Google.
  4. A site the AI is allowed to read.
- **What nobody can promise.** A specific AI mention. We can make you easier to find and understand, then measure it by asking the same questions every month in ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity.
- **Show, don't tell (walk-in tip).** Ask the AI live on your phone in front of the owner: "best [service] in [town]". Let them see who gets named. It lands harder than any printout, and the result may differ from the samples below, which is fine.

Method note for the samples below: two prompts per business, run through an AI search summary on 2026-11-10. These are snapshots, not a visibility study. Re-run them live in ChatGPT and Google AI before quoting a result to an owner.

  - No FAQ content and no llms.txt.

**AEO (Likely)**

**Pitch**

**Where AI can do a practical job**

- **Missed-call text-back**, so no emergency call goes to a competitor.
- **Turn tech notes into content.** AI drafts a short FAQ answer or Google post from each common repair ("why your AC blows warm air"). The owner approves before it goes up.
- Keep diagnosis and pricing with the techs.
