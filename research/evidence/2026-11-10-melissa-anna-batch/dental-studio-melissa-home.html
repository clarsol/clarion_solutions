Our Dental Services

Dr. Lena Khan, General Dentist, and our team care for children and adults in one comfortable office, from routine checkups to replacing missing teeth. Choose a service below to learn what to expect.

Dental Implants

Replace one missing tooth or several with an implant that looks, feels and works like a natural tooth. Dr. Khan places and restores implants in our office, and offers All-on-X for patients who need a full arch of fixed teeth.

Explore Dental Implants

Invisalign

Straighten crooked or crowded teeth and close gaps with clear, removable aligners instead of brackets and wires. Invisalign works for teens and adults, and most treatment takes 12 to 18 months.

Explore Invisalign

Emergency Dentistry

Severe toothache, a chipped or knocked-out tooth, or a lost crown? Call (469) 505-3055 and we will see you the same day whenever possible to relieve pain and protect the tooth.

Get emergency dental care

Cosmetic Dentistry

Brighten, reshape or repair your smile with porcelain veneers, professional teeth whitening and smile makeovers. Dr. Khan designs each treatment to suit your face and natural tooth color.

Explore cosmetic dentistry

Family Dentistry

One dental home for the whole family, from a child’s first checkup to care for parents and grandparents. We offer sealants and fluoride for kids, plus fillings, crowns and gum care for adults.

Explore family dentistry

Exams & Cleanings

Twice-yearly checkups and professional cleanings remove the plaque and tartar that brushing misses, and catch decay or gum disease early while it is simple to treat. Low-radiation digital x-rays show what an exam alone can’t.

Explore exams & cleanings

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions patients ask us most, from insurance and financing to your first visit.

How can I book an appointment?

Call or text us at 469-505-3055, or book online at flexbook.me/mds

Are you open on weekends?

Yes, we are open two Saturdays per month.

Are you open in the evenings?

Yes, we offer evening appointments.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept all PPO insurance plans.

Do you accept Medicare or Medicaid?

Original Medicare does not cover routine dental care. Many Medicare Advantage plans include PPO dental benefits, which we can accept. Texas Medicaid and CHIP dental plans are managed-care plans rather than PPO plans, so please call us at (469) 505-3055 before booking and we will check your coverage.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment options through Cherry, CareCredit, and Sunbit.

How much does Invisalign cost?

Invisalign ranges from $5,000 to $6,500, depending on your case (you also may have ortho coverage through insurance).

How much do veneers cost?

Veneers typically cost around $1,800 per tooth.

Do you see kids?

Yes, we treat children as young as 3 months old.

Do you offer braces?

We offer clear aligners, but not traditional metal braces.
