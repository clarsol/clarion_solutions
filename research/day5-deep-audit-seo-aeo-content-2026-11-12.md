## 1. USA Body Shop (Melissa + Anna). Top content prospect

### Content audit (Verified 11-12)
- **Instagram @usabodyshop_:** 46 followers, 8 posts from Oct 2025 to Apr 27, 2026 (nothing in 6.5 months).
  - Content is real repair photos and video, which is exactly the right raw material.
  - Bio lists both shops: Melissa and "Anna, TX 5005 Private Rd".
- **TikTok:** "USA BODY SHOP IN MELISSA TX" (@zee19810, the owner's handle).
  - 14 videos, posted July 2022 to July 2023, then stopped.
  - **Each got 207 to 1,029 views with 93 followers**, so unplanned shop videos already find local viewers.
- **Facebook:** "USA Body Shop", 6 followers.
- **Website:** zero images. No links to any social account.
- **Google listing** (Melissa): has owner photos and a Videos tab (11-10).
- **What customers praise** (11-10 reviews): paint and color matching, insurance help, fair pricing, Zee by name. All of it is visual or story content.

**Content as focal point: yes.** The pitch writes itself:
- "Your TikToks got up to 1,000 views with no plan. Your Instagram stopped in April. Your website has no photos. Every car you fix is a before-and-after nobody sees."
- Content package: weekly before/after reels, paint-match videos, an "insurance claim walkthrough with Zee" video, and the same clips on Google, IG, TikTok, FB and the website gallery.

### SEO (from 11-10, unchanged)
One page of about 140 words; no meta, schema or images; every URL returns the homepage (soft 404s); fake review widget; title "USABODYSHOP LLC". Full list in the 11-10 file.

### AEO (Verified 11-12, Google AI Mode)

| Question | Result | What AI said |
|---|---|---|
| "best body shop in Melissa TX" | 2nd of 5 | Behind Classic Colors (same building, Ste A) |

- **AEO fixes:**

- **TikTok:** is @zee19810 Zee's personal account or the shop's? It's titled as the shop.

## 2. Magnolia Ridge Dentistry. Content exists; distribution is the gap

### Content audit (Verified 11-12)
- **Instagram:** 59K followers, 897 posts, reels several times a week (newest Nov 9).
  - Views range from 974 to 126K.
  - Dr. Schuster on camera, airway and kids' sleep topics, "Comment EXPAND" lead magnet (automated DM funnel).
  - **Bio says "Kids Airway Dentist | N Dallas" and "📍McKinney area", not Melissa.**
- **Facebook:** 19K followers. Recent posts are behind the login wall (Open).
- **YouTube:** 9 subscribers, 38 videos, **newest a year ago**. The reels that get 100K views on IG aren't going to YouTube Shorts, which Google owns and AI cites.
- **Google listing:** no owner photos, no posts, 0 of 8 newest reviews replied (re-checked 11-12).
- **Website:**
  - Blog: 12 posts dated July 21-22, 2025 (national lead-magnet style: "Natural Botox", "Perimenopause Sleep Crisis"; two titles with "AND AND" typos), then 1 post in Feb 2026.
  - /before-and-after-photos/ is 268 words, /patient-stories/ 299, /dental-video-library/ 357. Thin, given how much video she makes.

**Content as focal point: yes, reframed.** Online, she's a national airway influencer, and the Melissa practice beside it looks neglected. Pitch:
- "You already make content people watch. It just never reaches the places patients in Melissa look: your Google listing, YouTube, your website, and AI answers."
- Content package as **repurposing and distribution**:
  - Reels to YouTube Shorts, Google posts and photos.
  - Website video pages with transcripts and FAQs.
  - A local "Melissa" angle.
- Plus review replies.
- Sell this as amplifying her content, never as making it. She's clearly good at production.

### SEO (Verified 11-12, 59-page crawl, `magnolia-crawl.json`)
- **Strong:**
  - About 45 service pages with Melissa in the titles: sleep apnea, TMJ, frenectomy, myofunctional, pediatric airway, emergency, implants and more.
  - Dentist schema with reviews and geo; real 404s.
  - Pages refreshed site-wide on Nov 2.
- **Gaps:**
  - FAQ markup on only 2 of about 45 service pages. FAQs are what AI quotes.
  - /ppc/ and /fbthankyou/ (about 130 words each) and /404-not-found/ are in the sitemap with no noindex, so they're indexable junk.
  - Blog title typos ("AND AND") and ALL-CAPS titles.
  - 7 of 18 homepage images missing alt text.
  - No llms.txt.
- The /ppc/ page and Google Tag Manager suggest they've run paid ads. Ask whether a vendor manages the site (the Nov 2 site-wide update suggests one).
