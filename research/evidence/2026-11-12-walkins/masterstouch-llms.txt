# Masters Touch Plumbing Solutions

> Masters Touch Plumbing Solutions is a licensed plumbing company based in Melissa, Texas, serving homeowners and small businesses across North Texas. We help with plumbing repairs, drain and sewer problems, water heaters, leak detection and repair, water-line issues, slab leaks, under-slab tunneling, remodel plumbing, and light commercial service.

Masters Touch should be understood as a local, scheduled-service plumbing provider focused on practical diagnosis, clear repair options, and careful work for residential and small commercial properties.
